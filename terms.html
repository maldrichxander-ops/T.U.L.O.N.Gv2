About the system

T.U.L.O.N.G. (Tactical Unit Locator & Operations Network Grid) is a real-time map used to coordinate fire and rescue vehicles in Cebu City. It was developed by Aldrich Xander Morano and is maintained by the T.U.L.O.N.G Team. The system is for authorized administrators, dispatchers and vehicle operators.

Not an emergency line

T.U.L.O.N.G. is a coordination tool. It does not take emergency calls. If you are in an emergency, call 911 or your local fire and rescue hotline directly.

Accuracy & availability

Vehicle positions, distances, routes and arrival times are estimates. They depend on GPS signal, internet connection, vehicle hardware and outside services such as maps and routing, and they can be late, wrong or missing. Always use your own judgment and standard dispatch procedures.
