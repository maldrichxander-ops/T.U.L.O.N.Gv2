Privacy Policy

We follow the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). The person responsible for your data is Aldrich Xander Morano (contact below).

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, mobile number, profile picture (optional), role, and assigned unit or station.
  • Vehicle details: unit ID, vehicle type, plate number, station or agency, and the device (Blynk) token that links a vehicle to the system.
  • Location: the GPS position of each vehicle, and of an operator's phone if the operator chooses to share it.
  • Incident records: type, address and coordinates, classification, alarm level, who responded, and a time-stamped timeline of changes. Exported reports and an incident archive are kept.
  • Sign-in and device data: your sign-in session, and small settings saved in your browser, such as sound preferences. We do not use advertising trackers.

Names and addresses of people involved in an emergency are not required. Please do not enter more personal information in an incident than is needed for the response.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and give you the right access.
  • To show units and incidents on the map, find the nearest vehicle, and send dispatch alerts and routes.
  • To keep records and create end-of-day reports.
  • To keep the system secure and fix problems.

We do not sell your information and do not use it for advertising.

Your location

  • Vehicle GPS is sent by the unit's own device while it is online.
  • On a phone or tablet, the browser asks for your permission before location is shared. It is shared only while the operator page is open and sharing is on.
  • You can press Stop on the sharing bar, or turn off location permission in your browser or phone, at any time. Logging out also stops it.

Services we use

To work, T.U.L.O.N.G. relies on outside services. They receive only what is needed for their part and handle it under their own policies.

  • Google Firebase: sign-in and the database that stores accounts, vehicles, incidents and live locations.
  • Blynk: the link between the vehicle devices (ESP32) and the dashboards.
  • OpenStreetMap: map tiles and address search (Nominatim).
  • OSRM and OpenRouteService: route and arrival-time calculation. These receive the start and end coordinates of a route.
  • Google Fonts and public code libraries (unpkg, cdnjs): load page fonts and scripts. Like any website you visit, they can see your IP address and browser type.

Who can see it

  • Administrators can see all units, incidents and operator accounts.
  • Operators can see the live map, incidents and other units, and their own profile.
  • We may disclose information if the law or a government authority requires it, or to protect someone's safety.

Keeping & security

Account information is kept while the account is active. Incident records and reports are kept in the archive for operational and reporting purposes until an administrator deletes them, or until you ask us to remove personal details we can lawfully remove.

Access requires sign-in, is limited by role, and the database is protected by security rules. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong password and log out on shared devices.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you may ask to see, correct, block or delete your personal data, object to how it is used, and receive a copy. To use any of these rights, email us (below). If you believe your data was mishandled, you may also complain to the National Privacy Commission.

This system is for adults who work or volunteer in emergency response and is not meant for children under 18. If we update this policy, the date at the top will change.

Support

Questions about your data, or a problem with your account? Write to us and we will reply as soon as we can.
