Operator Manual
How to use the T.U.L.O.N.G. Operator Dashboard — for drivers and responders.

Welcome, Operator

T.U.L.O.N.G. — Tactical Unit Locator & Network Grid. This dashboard shows you the live map, your vehicle, and every emergency the dispatcher logs.

The operator dashboard layout

What you can do here

  • Receive dispatch calls from the admin with the incident type, address and a route.
  • Watch your unit’s status (Standby / Responding / Arrived) and GPS.
  • See all active incidents and stations on the map, and open the incident log.
  • Update an incident you are handling (classification, alarm level) and resolve it.
  • Share your phone’s live location with the dispatcher.
  • Edit your profile and change your password in Settings.
Operators cannot create, delete or dispatch incidents — the admin does that. Your own vehicle is registered by the admin, so you do not need to set anything up.

Header

  • Live clock and date, your name and unit (click to edit your profile), Settings, and Logout.

Dispatch Calls

When the admin sends your unit to an emergency, a full-screen alert appears and an alarm sounds.

What you see when the admin calls your unit

What you will see and hear

  • A red dispatch screen with a timer, the incident type (with its classification and alarm level), the address, the distance from you, and your unit ID.
  • A siren with a spoken voice message, and vibration on phones.

What to do

  1. Press the RESPOND button on your vehicle’s device (ESP32). This is the only thing that stops the alarm.
  2. Optionally tap View location & route to hide the screen and fly the map to the incident. The alarm keeps reminding you until you press RESPOND on the device.
  3. A blue route to the incident is drawn automatically, with distance and ETA, and it refreshes as you move.
  4. When you reach the scene, press ARRIVED on the device. The route is cleared and an Arrived banner is shown.
The timer turns urgent after 80 seconds (the turnout-time target). If there is no response, the alarm gets louder in bursts every minute until RESPOND is pressed.
No sound? Tap the “Tap to enable emergency sound” button that appears at the bottom-left. Browsers only allow audio after you have touched the page once, so do this at the start of every shift.

My Vehicle & Status

The My Vehicle card in the side panel shows your unit’s details and current status.

Unit status — set by the physical buttons on your ESP32 device
ItemMeaning
Unit ID / Type / StationYour registered vehicle (set by the admin).
GPSYour unit’s coordinates, or No Fix if the GPS has no signal yet.
StandbyOnline and available.
RespondYou are on the way to an incident.
ArrivedYou are at the scene.
The three status boxes only show your state — you change it with the physical STANDBY / RESPONDING / ARRIVED buttons on your ESP32 device. If your device is off, your unit appears offline and cannot receive routes.

On the map, vehicle pins are red for firetrucks and blue for ambulances; the label color under the pin shows the status (green, orange or red).

Incidents on the Map

Every active emergency appears on the map as a red (fire) or blue (medical) marker, shared live with the admin.

Open an incident

Tap or click a marker to open its popup showing the address and controls.

  • Fire: set the Classification (e.g. Residential, Grass fire, False Alarm) and the Alarm Level (Fire on Progress, 1st–5th Alarm, Task Force, General Alarm, Under Control, Fire Out).
  • Medical: set the Medical Emergency Type (e.g. Vehicular Accident, Drowning, Suspected Stroke). Choose Other to type a specific emergency.
  • Resolve & Save closes the incident when the situation is over. The marker turns grey. Choosing Fire Out resolves it automatically.

Changes are time-stamped in the incident timeline and appear instantly on the admin dashboard.

Only resolve an incident when the response is truly finished — the admin sees it as closed and may export it to the daily report.

Incident log

Open the Events tab to see all incidents, newest first, with filters All / Active / Resolved. Tap an entry to jump to it on the map.

Side Panel & Counters

The floating panel toggle and the location-sharing bar
  • Status ribbon (top): counts of online firetrucks and ambulances, active fire and medical incidents, and stations.
  • Units tab: your vehicle card and the live units list. Tap a unit to fly to it. Units that are offline are greyed out.
  • Events tab: the incident log (see Incidents on the Map).
  • Stations tab: fire stations, volunteer brigades and ambulance/rescue teams. Tap one to see it on the map; its popup shows the hotline number. A yellow ≈ means the pin is approximate.
  • The round orange button floating on the map hides or shows the whole side panel to give the map more room.
  • The Live Tracking box on the map can be collapsed with its small toggle.
  • The sync bar at the bottom-left shows the time of the last successful update from your units.

Sharing My Location (phones)

On a phone or tablet, you can let the admin see exactly where you are during a response.

  1. When the dashboard opens, a prompt asks “Share your live location?” Tap Allow location, then allow it again in your phone’s permission popup.
  2. A bar at the bottom shows Sharing live location with your accuracy (± metres) and the last update time.
  3. Tap Stop on that bar any time to stop sharing; tap Turn on to start again.
  • Your location is shared only while this page is open and sharing is on. Logging out also stops it.
  • Your position appears as a blue dot on your own map.
  • If you tapped Not now, use the bottom bar’s Turn on button later.
  • Keep the screen on and the page open for continuous updates.

Settings & Password

Click your name badge or the Settings button in the header.

  • Profile picture — choose a photo (PNG, JPG, WEBP or GIF); it is resized automatically. You can also remove it.
  • Full name and Mobile number — editable. The phone number may contain digits, spaces, + - and brackets.
  • Email (login) and Unit / Station — read-only; ask the admin if they need to change.
  • Change password — enter your current password, then the new one twice (minimum 6 characters).

Press Save to apply. A green message confirms the change.

Session & Security

  • Use Logout (header) when your shift ends — this also stops location sharing.
  • Never share your password. Change it in Settings if you think someone else knows it.
  • Notifications and alarms need the page to stay open, so do not close this tab while on duty.

Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to check
No alarm when dispatchedTap the sound-enable button, raise your volume, and make sure the tab is open and not muted.
My unit shows offlineCheck your ESP32 has power and Wi-Fi/data. It reconnects by itself.
No route shownPress RESPONDING on the device and wait for a GPS fix. If routing is unavailable, a dashed straight line is shown.
“No GPS fix yet”Move to open sky; the route appears when a location is found.
Admin cannot see my phoneCheck the bottom bar says Sharing live location; allow location in your browser/phone settings.
Red sync banner / “unit list unavailable”Check your internet. If it stays, tell the admin so the database permissions can be checked.
