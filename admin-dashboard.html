How to use the Admin Dispatch Dashboard — its functions and features.

What is T.U.L.O.N.G.?

Tactical Unit Locator & Network Grid — a real-time map for dispatching fire trucks and ambulances in Cebu and Mactan.

The admin dashboard is the control center. From here you can log emergencies on the map, watch registered vehicles (ESP32 units) move live, send a unit to an incident, talk to units through the terminal, register new vehicles and operator accounts, and export end-of-day reports.

Screen layout

  • Left sidebar — Report Emergency button, live counters, the list of live units, report/export buttons, Register Emergency Vehicle, About/Contact links, Terminate Session, and the list of fire stations and rescue teams.
  • Map (center) — incidents, units and stations. Use the search box (top-left of the map) to find a street in Cebu/Mactan. The Live Tracking panel (top-right) shows how many firetrucks, ambulances and incidents are active.
  • Nearby Units panel (right) — opens when you choose an incident; used to dispatch units.
  • ESP32 Command Terminal (bottom) — click its bar to expand or collapse.
  • ? button (header) — opens this manual.

Sidebar counters

On the small 7″ touchscreen, the sidebar is hidden behind the MENU button at the top-left.

Reporting an Emergency

Put a new incident on the map. It appears instantly for every admin and operator.

  1. Press Report Emergency in the sidebar. The button turns yellow and the cursor becomes a crosshair.
  2. Click the exact location on the map. The address is looked up automatically.
  3. In the popup, choose FIRE or MEDICAL.

The incident gets an ID like INC-20261111-00001, starts as For Verification, and the Nearby Units panel opens so you can dispatch right away.

Changed your mind? Press the yellow button again to cancel, or just close the popup — nothing is saved until you pick FIRE or MEDICAL.

Managing Incidents

Click any incident marker to open its control popup.

Fire incidents

  • Classification — For Verification, False Alarm, Rubbish Fire, Post Fire, Vehicle fire, Grass fire, Residential, Commercial.
  • Alarm Level — from Fire out upon arrival / Fire on Progress up through 1st–5th Alarm, Task Force Alpha to India, General Alarm, Under Control, and Fire Out. The current tactical level is shown as a label under the marker.
  • Choosing Fire Out automatically resolves the incident after a moment.

Medical incidents

  • Medical Emergency Type — Suspected Heart Attack / Cardiac Arrest, Suspected Stroke, Vehicular Accident, Drowning, Possible Suicide, Falling Incident, Fire Incident, Electrocution, or Other.
  • For Other, type the specific emergency in the box that appears.

Buttons in the popup

ButtonWhat it does
Nearby Units & DispatchOpens the dispatch panel for this incident.
Resolve & SaveMarks the incident resolved (marker turns grey, routes are removed). It stays on the map until you export it.
DeleteRemoves the incident completely. Use for mistakes only — it is not saved to any report.

Every status or alarm change is written to the incident’s timeline with a time stamp, which is included in the Excel report.

Nearby Units & Dispatch

Find the closest online vehicles and send them to an incident.

  1. Click an incident, then Nearby Units & Dispatch (it also opens automatically for incidents you just created).
  2. Pick a Search radius (3, 5, 10, 20 or 50 km). Only units that are online and have a GPS fix are listed, nearest first, with distance and a rough ETA.
  3. Press Respond beside a unit — or Respond Nearest Unit to automatically pick the closest unit that is not already responding.
  4. The button changes to Sent. The unit’s operator dashboard rings and shows the incident location and address.

What Respond really does

  • Sends the text UNIT-ID Respond to that unit’s ESP32 (shown in the terminal).
  • Saves a dispatch alert that the operator dashboard picks up.
  • When the unit presses its RESPONDING button, a blue route to the incident is drawn automatically (with distance and ETA) and refreshes as the unit moves.

Use Show incident on map to fly back to the incident, and the UNITS tab on the panel’s left edge to hide or show the panel.

Live Units (ESP32)

Every registered vehicle reports its status and GPS location through Blynk, refreshed every 5 seconds.

StatusMarker / badgeMeaning
STANDBYGreenOnline and available.
RESPONDINGOrangeOn the way to an incident (route is drawn).
ARRIVEDRedAt the scene (route is cleared).
OFFLINEGreyed in the listDevice not connected, or no valid token. It is removed from the map.
  • Pin colors — red pin = firetruck, blue pin = ambulance.
  • Click a unit card in the sidebar to fly to it; click its marker for coordinates.
  • A unit with no GPS fix shows No GPS fix and cannot be dispatched until it gets one.
  • Going online, offline, and every button press are logged in the terminal.

ESP32 Command Terminal

Send text to your vehicles and read what they send back (Blynk virtual pin V3).

  1. Click the terminal bar at the bottom to expand it.
  2. Choose ALL UNITS or one specific unit in the dropdown.
  3. Type your message and press Enter or SEND.
TagMeaning
[TX >>]Message you sent.
[ACK]Delivered to Blynk / unit came online.
[RX <<]Message or button press received from a unit.
[ERR]Failure, unit offline, or missing token.
[SYS]System information.

The terminal opens by itself when a unit goes online/offline or sends a message.

Register Emergency Vehicle

Add a new ESP32 unit and create the driver’s login in one step.

  1. Press Register Emergency Vehicle in the sidebar.
  2. Fill in Unit ID (e.g. A-004), Vehicle Type, Plate Number, Station / Agency, and the Blynk Token of the ESP32 (Blynk Console → Device → Device Info).
  3. Fill in the operator’s Full Name, Email and a Password (minimum 6 characters).
  4. Press Register Unit & Create Account.

The Blynk token is checked first, duplicate Unit IDs are rejected, and your admin session is not affected. On success, a summary of the login is shown: the operator signs in at tulong.services/operator-dashboard with that email and password.

Copy the credentials from the summary before closing the window — the password is not shown again. Share it with the operator privately.

Reports & History

Export incidents to Excel and keep a permanent archive.

ButtonWhat it does
Export EOD ReportDownloads an Excel file of resolved incidents only, then clears them from the map. Active incidents stay.
Wipe Map & ExportDownloads a report of everything (active + resolved) after a confirmation, then clears the whole map.
Report HistoryLists every past export. Download a copy again or Show on map to view archived incidents (press Hide on the chip to remove them).

The workbook has sheets for All Incidents, Fire Incidents and Medical Incidents with ID, location, coordinates, reported/resolved time, duration, classification, final alarm and the progression timeline.

Incidents are copied to the archive before being cleared, so nothing is lost if a download fails. If Excel cannot be created, a CSV copy is downloaded instead.
Always export before pressing Terminate Session or wiping the map.

Stations & Map

  • The Fire Stations & Rescue list in the sidebar shows BFP stations (red-orange border), volunteer brigades (blue) and ambulance/rescue teams (green). Click one to fly to it and see its hotline number.
  • A yellow ≈ next to a station means its pin is approximate — verify before relying on it.
  • The map search box finds streets inside the Cebu/Mactan coverage area; results outside it are rejected.
  • Scroll or pinch to zoom; drag to move around.
  • The Blynk sync bar at the bottom-left shows the time of the last successful unit update (green dot = at least one unit reachable).

Notifications

  • When an incident is reported from another dashboard, a card slides in on the left with the address and a View on map button, plus a short alert tone. New-emergency cards stay until dismissed.
  • When an incident is resolved, a green card appears and clears itself after 15 seconds.
  • If the browser tab is in the background, a desktop notification is shown (allow notifications when asked).
  • Sound and notifications become active after your first click or key press on the page (a browser rule).
  • A red banner under the header means incident sync is failing; it retries automatically.

Session & Security

  • This page is admin only. Signed-out visitors are sent to the login page, and operators are sent to their own dashboard.
  • For safety you are signed out automatically after 15 minutes of inactivity (no click, key press, scroll or mouse movement).
  • Use Terminate Session to log out manually. Download your EOD report first.
  • Never share the admin password. Operators only ever need their own login.
  • About Us and Contact Us (sidebar) open in a new tab so your live map keeps running.
